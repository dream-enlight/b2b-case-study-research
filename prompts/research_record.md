A complete record contains provider, source_url, customer, people, company_profile,
buying_situation, status and note. Preserve assigned provider and source URL.
Extract customer/stakeholder facts only from the published case and its linked
full-case document; no enrichment through social profiles, company pages or
unrelated articles.

People: preserve names, actual titles/functions and evidenced involvement. Separate
evaluation, selection, implementation, operation, testimonials and authorship. A
quote or job title does not establish purchasing authority; generic descriptions
are not formal titles. Missing involvement or authority remains unknown.
Company profile: explain what the customer sells/does and the relevant business
and integration context, preserving meaningful detail and ambiguities.
Buying situation: capture the prior approach, original problems/pressures, events
and buying trigger, goals/context, requirements, alternatives and attributed
selection reasons where stated. Keep the adoption chronology distinct from later
benefits and outcomes. Preserve evidence associations for multiple customers on
one source. Mark unavailable facts and uncertainty explicitly. Use enough source
specific detail for later analysis without reopening the story; do not provide GTM
recommendations, frequency analysis or report writing.

Substantively read relevant cases require the complete extraction now. A partial
landing page with an unattempted full case/PDF may instead queue needs_review if
relevance remains uncertain: capture that document's actual link for reuse. A
failed full-case access is unresolved, not another review request. Excluded and
unresolved screening decisions have record=null.
