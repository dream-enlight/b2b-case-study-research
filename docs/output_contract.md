# Public output contract

No private example workbook or report is required. Categories emerge from the finalized evidence rather than from an example industry.

## Evidence Sheet

One native `Case studies` tab, exactly five columns:

| Column | Evidence to preserve |
| --- | --- |
| Case-study source | Provider and original source URL. |
| Customer company | Source-supported customer identity. |
| People involved | Names, actual titles and documented involvement; distinguish evaluation, selection, implementation, operation, testimonial and authorship. Unknown function and authority remain unknown. |
| Company profile | What the company sells or does, its business model and relevant context. |
| Buying situation | Prior approach, pressures/triggers, goals, stated requirements, alternatives, selection reasons, documented capabilities/integrations/workflows and outcomes. Keep chronology and uncertainty explicit. |

Unknown buying motivation must not erase known usage. A product use is not automatically a stated requirement or original purchase reason. Preserve details in self-contained evidence without reopening sources during analysis. Complete accessible cases appear in the Sheet; inaccessible cases and reasons remain local.

## Native Doc tabs

**Overview:** concise one-page-style summary of Company profiles, People involved, Buying situations and a methodological caveat. No strategy recommendations.

**Company profiles:** complete exclusive table `Company type | What it sells / does | Count | Share`, including nonzero Other and Unknown. Explain actual businesses, meaningful differences and provider concentration.

**People:** `Functional role group | Representative actual titles | Count | Share`. Derive functions conservatively from supported titles. Evaluation/selection/implementation/testimonial participation is separate from function and authority. Keep named people whose function is unknown. Use the same counting authority for the table and Overview coverage.

**Buying situations:** retain separate pressures/triggers, goals, deployment context, requirements/documented usage and outcomes. Use the existing kind/evidence fields. Requirements and documented usage may share a capability category, but must retain distinct labels and profile unions. Include customer examples and chronology caveats.

**Methods & sources:** provider coverage and source associations; eligible entries versus distinct profiles; identity rules, overlaps, missing evidence, inaccessible sources and publication bias. Explain what the dataset can and cannot establish.

Python computes unique-profile unions. Primary company types are exclusive; situations, roles and evidence kinds overlap. Use the overall preserved-profile denominator for profile shares, eligible entry counts for provider coverage, and explicit coverage figures for missing functions. Do not silently weight, exclude or infer authority. Separate observations, derived counts and qualitative interpretation. Preserve useful depth and defensible frequencies without arbitrary word limits.
